**Three months in, and I'm still learning which building is which.**

Going from a helpdesk supporting a rotating cast of managed customers to being embedded full time in a single organisation is a bigger adjustment than I expected. At McArdles, every ticket came with a different company attached, a different set of quirks, a different point of contact. Here it's one organisation, but a much bigger and more varied one than a single office: seven sites around Dublin plus a handful of national education hubs, staff and learners rather than just staff, and a mission that actually shapes how decisions get made day to day.

That last part took some adjusting to. A helpdesk ticket at a managed customer is usually just a technical problem. Here, a request is just as often tangled up with someone's course, their access to education, their route back into employment. It changes how I think about priority. A slow laptop is still a slow laptop, but the person waiting on it might be trying to finish an assignment before a deadline that matters more to them than it would in a typical office.

The actual work has ramped up quickly. I'm doing hands-on Microsoft 365 and Entra ID administration now in a way I only touched occasionally before, account lifecycle, permissions, multifactor authentication rollout, the information governance side of things that never made it into a McArdles ticket. Contributing to cybersecurity controls properly, rather than just applying someone else's policy, has been the steepest part of the learning curve.

Mostly, though, it's been about learning the place. Which site has which quirks in its network cabling. Which staff member is the actual go-to for a given department, regardless of what the org chart says. What "the system's down" usually turns out to mean once you dig into it. None of that shows up on a CV, but it's the difference between fixing a ticket and actually being useful to the people around you.

Early days still, but it already feels less like a new job and more like the start of something I want to be good at.
