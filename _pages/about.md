IT professional with continuous experience since March 2020, currently leading day-to-day IT operations, infrastructure and service desk, for a multi-site organisation supporting around 500 to 600 users and 310 devices. I build automation, workflows and integrations around how an organisation already works: understanding a process with the people who use it before redesigning it, rather than imposing change from outside.

## Core Skills

- **Microsoft cloud and identity:** Microsoft 365, Entra ID (Azure AD), Intune, Autopilot, Exchange Online, SharePoint, Teams, Conditional Access, MFA
- **Infrastructure and networking:** Windows Server, on-prem Active Directory, Cisco switches, structured cabling, VLANs/ACLs, firewalls (Fortinet, Sophos, UniFi)
- **Virtualisation and recovery:** Hyper-V clustering, live migration and replication, VMware/Proxmox exposure, backup, disaster recovery, documented RTO/RPO
- **Service delivery:** ITIL-aligned incident/problem/change management, ticketing and PSA tooling, SLA ownership, root-cause investigation, vendor evaluation
- **Leadership:** team coaching and mentoring, performance management, stakeholder communication, senior-leadership reporting

**The short version:** I don't want to impose process, I want to sit with the people who use it. A short "day in the life" or a role swap tells you more than a requirements document alone. It's the same instinct behind every project I take on: build the tool around the workflow, not the other way round.

## Outside of Work

When I'm not knee-deep in infrastructure, I'm usually gaming with friends, running live AV for an event, or turning a CAD model into something real on the 3D printer. That same habit of understanding how something actually works before I touch it carries over from work into hobbies more than I'd probably like to admit.

So when I want a proper break, I go the other way entirely: pack a tent and head off-grid for a few days with no signal and no screens. It's the fastest reset I've found, and I come back sharper for it.
